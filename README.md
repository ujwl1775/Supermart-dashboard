1. Project Overview
Ram, a data analyst at Supermart, is asked to turn the company's sales data into a simple, interactive dashboard. The goal is to let the management team answer important business questions quickly, without having to go through the complete dataset.
2. Business Problem
Management needs a single place to monitor sales, profit, product performance, discounts and location-wise performance, so that decisions on pricing, discounting, stocking and regional focus can be made with evidence.
3. Objectives and Key Questions
Objective	Business question	Tasks
1. Overall Sales Performance	How is the overall grocery business performing?	Total sales and total profit; categories with the largest share of sales; sales trend over time; regions with the highest sales
2. Product Performance	Which products and categories are driving sales and profit?	Categories and sub-categories with the highest sales; products with high sales but comparatively low profit
3. Profit and Discount	How are discounts and profit related to business performance?	Categories and regions with the highest profit; relationship between discount and profit
4. Geographic Performance	Which locations are contributing most to the business?	States and cities with the highest sales; locations with high sales but comparatively low profit
